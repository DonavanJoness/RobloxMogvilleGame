That would make him 20 on October 28, 2026. This is source-corroborated biographical information, not verification of a particular photo's date or owner.

The Know Your Meme article links the public [Instagram profile](https://www.instagram.com/clavicular0/) and [Kick channel](https://kick.com/clavicular). No authentic adult-era portrait with documented commercial reuse rights was selected. “Young” photos must be dated; content from 2021 would have been made while he was under 18 and should not be used for this adult-only roster.

## GigaChad / Ernest Khalimov

[Know Your Meme: GigaChad](https://knowyourmeme.com/memes/gigachad) was fetched successfully. It associates the meme with `berlin.1969` / Ernest Khalimov and attributes the photographic art project to **Krista Sudmalis**, **Sleek'N'Tears**. Its description explicitly calls the imagery Photoshopped photographs involving several models.

That distinction matters for this game's authentic-photo requirement: an iconic edited meme image must not be silently labelled an unaltered real portrait. A cleared photographic artwork could be labelled accurately as artwork, but that would need the owner's approval under the requested art policy.

The source links [Sleek'N'Tears](https://sleekntears.com/) and the [berlin.1969 Instagram profile](https://www.instagram.com/berlin.1969/). The HTTPS robots request to the former returned a proxy tunnel 403, so the creator website's contents, contact details and license terms were not retrieved. The Instagram profile was not fetched. These links are permission leads only. No commercial image reuse or game-likeness grant was documented, and no images were downloaded.

`https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/GigaChad` returned 404. A subject expressing enjoyment of memes or a template being widely shared does not establish commercial photo rights.
